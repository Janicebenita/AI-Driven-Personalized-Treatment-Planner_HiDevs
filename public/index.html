From patient context to a reviewable plan

  1. Validate & triage. Check input ranges and scope. Acute symptoms, pregnancy, paediatric cases and unsupported diagnoses withhold routine plans.
  2. Retrieve evidence. Sparse TF-IDF vectors and cosine similarity rank a curated NICE, KDIGO and CPIC corpus. This is vector search, not neural semantic search. Similarity is not clinical certainty.
  3. Apply safety rules. Kidney function, selected medicines, verified SLCO1B1 phenotype, frailty and incomplete information produce explicit signals.
  4. Synthesise optionally. A configured Groq/Llama or OpenAI model provides a separate, schema-validated briefing using only the supplied evidence. It cannot alter the deterministic options or safety gates. Unconfigured deployments run in clearly labelled rules-only mode.
  5. Review with a clinician. Export a report with input context, evidence IDs, limitations and monitoring. No automatic sign-off or prescription.

Known boundaries

This is an unvalidated research prototype for adult type 2 diabetes. The corpus is curated, not a live literature search. Drug interactions, allergy matching and diagnoses are not comprehensive. No production healthcare privacy or regulatory certification is claimed. Use synthetic cases only.

Evidence provenance

Original brief summaries reference published guidelines. The corpus was assembled on 14 November 2026. Guideline sections and local drug labels must be verified at point of care. Monitoring intervals other than cited HbA1c reviews are clinician-set.
